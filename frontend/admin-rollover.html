Step 1: Create New Academic Year

This creates the new year in an inactive state. The current year remains active until Step 6.

Loading preview...

Step 2: Auto-Create Offerings

Creates offerings for every module in every program's curriculum for the new year.

Step 3: Promote Students

Advances all non-final-year students to the next year and auto-enrolls them in new semester-1 modules.

Final-year students are skipped — they are handled in Step 4.

Step 4: Graduate Final-Year Students

Marks eligible final-year students as Graduated and computes their award classification.

Step 5: Seed Assessments

Adds the standard 3 assessments (Quiz 20% + Assignment 30% + Final Exam 50%) to every new offering.

Step 6: Activate New Year

The final atomic switch. Deactivates the old year, activates the new year. Students will now see their new modules.

This is the final step. Make sure Steps 1-5 are all complete. Once activated, the previous year becomes read-only.

Emergency Rollback

If a rollover went wrong, you can undo it here. This deletes the year you specify, all its semesters, offerings, and enrollments, and re-activates the previous year.
